ButterGlow – Third-party licenses
================================

ButterGlow itself is released under the MIT License (see LICENSE).

The browser extension bundles the components listed below. Each keeps its own
license; the full license texts follow. Tools that are only used to build and
test the project (esbuild, puppeteer-core and their dependencies) are not part
of the extension and are not listed here.


Overview
--------

  Component            Version         License   Used for
  -------------------  --------------  --------  ----------------------------------
  butterchurn          3.0.0-beta.5    MIT       Milkdrop rendering engine (WebGL)
  butterchurn-presets  3.0.0-beta.4    MIT       Milkdrop presets and textures
  eel-wasm             0.0.16          MIT       Compiles preset equations to WebAssembly


Milkdrop presets
----------------

The presets in butterchurn-presets were created by many authors of the
MilkDrop community (for example Geiss, martin, Flexi, Rovastar, Zylot and
others). Their names are part of each preset's title. The collection is
distributed by Jordan Berg under the MIT License below; the individual presets
remain the creative work of their authors.

Presets saved by the preset editor whose file name ends in "(bearbeitet)" are
edited versions of these presets. The original title and author are kept in the file
name and in the "butterchurn_based_on" entry inside the file.
